Plot y=f(x) in 2D; plot z=f(x,y) alone or pair it with y=f(x) to highlight a curve in 3D. Supported: polynomials, rational functions, trig, logs, powers, abs, min/max, and if(condition, true, false).
Supported functions and interactions

2D: y=x^2, y=1/(x-1), y=sin(3x), y=exp(-x^2), or y=if(x<0,-x,x). Separate curves by newline. 3D: z=sin(sqrt(x^2+y^2)), z=x^2-y^2, or the paired lines y=x×x and z=sin(x×y). Paired equations draw the surface and highlight its constrained 3D curve (x, x², sin(x³)). A single y=f(x) is drawn as a curve on the z=0 plane. Rotate by dragging, use Pan 3D or Shift + drag to move the view, and scroll to zoom toward the pointer. Click Axes & grid to set X/Y/Z bounds and grid spacing. Multiple surfaces can be entered on separate lines as z=..., z2=... and z3=.... Radian trig notation is used for graphs.
