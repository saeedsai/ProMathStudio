Engineering assumptions

Small-displacement, linear-elastic, static 2D pin-connected bars with axial stiffness EA/L. Fixed supports have zero prescribed displacement. The optional member-level Euler estimate uses Pcr = π²EI/(KL)² when both I (second moment of area in mm⁴) and K (dimensionless effective-length factor) are supplied. After solving, only compressive axial force is compared with Pcr, reporting utilization Pcomp/Pcr and load ratio Pcr/Pcomp. Tension is marked not applicable and zero axial force is reported as no compression demand without division. This ideal elastic screen does not model geometric buckling or account for imperfections, residual stress, local/flexural-torsional buckling, yielding, connection behavior or design-code resistance. The linear truss solver itself remains axial-only and does not include geometric buckling. This is a learning aid, not a certified structural design check.

A numerical mechanism or unrestrained direction is rejected. Inspect residual force balance and energy verification before using results.
